# Cross-formation comparison of the frozen Medium decisions

Every decision is compared with the best available spacing of each of the other four formations, under the same wind and charging-pad count. A separate matched-spacing CSV compares formations at the selected spacing wherever real eligible data exist. Neither design substitutes missing or excluded profiles. Summed Bideal SOC drop is a normalized battery-consumption metric, not a measured energy value in joules. P1-P5 identify the recorded positions within each formation; equal position numbers need not have equal aerodynamic roles. The geometry discussion is a mechanistic interpretation of the measured profiles, not a decomposition of their motor power.
